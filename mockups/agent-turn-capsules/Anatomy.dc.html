One 44px row replaces the whole inlined tool log. Everything the agent did sits behind it; the reply you are meant to read never does.

Motion

  • Expand: grid-template-rows 0fr → 1fr, 380 ms, ease-out — no measured heights.
  • Steps cascade in, 55 ms stagger, 6 px slide.
  • Success checks draw themselves (stroke-dashoffset).
  • Reply slides up 8 px after the capsule settles.
  • Everything collapses to instant under prefers-reduced-motion.

Rules

  • Collapsed by default, including the turn in progress.
  • The final reply renders outside the capsule, always.
  • Text is “final” only if nothing follows it in the turn. Earlier text is narration: a said step inside the capsule, set in prose, and the live shimmer line quotes the latest one.
  • Subagent steps nest their own calls one level deep; deeper goes to the Subagents tab.
  • Long commands truncate with ellipsis; hover a step for the full line.
  • Dot colours: bash · read · edit · ask · subagent · thinking · said (square)
