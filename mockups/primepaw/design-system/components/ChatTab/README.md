# ChatTab

Folder-style tabs across the top of the chat sheet.

One `pp-tab` button per view inside `pp-tabs`. The selected tab (`is-on`, `aria-current="page"`) rises 1px, turns to `sheet-warm` and gets a `red` top edge; the others sit 2px lower on `paper-2` with a `shadow-tab` on their right edge. Hover lifts a tab 2px in two steps. Tabs scroll horizontally when they do not fit; never wrap them.
