# Characters

The faces of the main agent. The pet’s three characters are PNG stickers, 176px square, each a round head with a white die-cut rim and no shadow: `robot-face.png` (the robot dog, the one every board wears), `dog-face.png` (the puppy) and `cat-face.png` (the cat). Show one with `kr-pface` as a background image: 64px as the sticker, 32px as a thread portrait or on the Meet your Main Agent strip, 26px on the `kr-ib kr-jarvis` tile, 24px on a small photo, 16px on a row. The peeking sprites and the standee sheets live with the Primepaw mockup under `pet/`.

`jarvis.svg` and `jarvis-face.svg` are Button, the cut-out cat that was Jarvis before the pet: the cut-out that peeked over the composer and his face as a flat sticker, the drawn style the other agents’ portraits keep. See the `Jarvis` component.
