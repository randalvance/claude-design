# Jarvis

The main agent is a cat called Button, and Button is a paper cut-out. He is the one character in Primepaw; everything else is paper, cardboard and stickers.

## The cut

A round cream head with a kraft patch over his right ear and down that side, two pointed ears, bead eyes, blush cheeks, a red nose, two whiskers a side, and a red bow tie at the chin. Two paws hang below, so he can hold on to an edge. He is outlined in 1.6px ink and cut from cream (#f3e9d5) and kraft (#d9bc86); the bow and the nose are the red (#c8553d), the cheeks blush (#e8a9a2) at 70%. The drawing is `assets/Characters/jarvis.svg`, on an 84 by 70 box. `kr-jarvis-cut` gives it the paper's cut edge: a 1px light rim by day, white at night, and a hair of shadow.

## Where he is

- **Over the composer.** `kr-jarvis-peek`: 92px wide, 28px in from the right, leaning 4 degrees about his paws, which rest on the composer's frame. Below 860px he goes.
- **Over the greeting.** The empty home's hello sheet has him at 84px, 12px in from the right.
- **The portrait.** His face as a flat sticker (`assets/Characters/jarvis-face.svg`, a 64 by 64 box): the same drawing in the same paper colours, the kraft patch, bead eyes, the red nose and the red bow, with a white die-cut rim and no shadow. It is never pixel art. It is the cream `kr-ib kr-jarvis` tile that tops every rail, at 32px, and the first photo in the Meet your Main Agent strip. A main agent can be given another animal's portrait there, and then the tile shows that one; the cut-out over the composer stays Button.

## What he does not do

He is a cut-out, not a character animation: he never blinks, bounces, pulses or follows the pointer. The paper's lift on hover and the stickers' land are the only motion near him, and they are not his.
