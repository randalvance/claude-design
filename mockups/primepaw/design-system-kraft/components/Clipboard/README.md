# Clipboard

`kr-clipboard` is cardboard with a paper clip cut from dyed card (`kr-clipboard-clip`) that holds what the agent made. Its head is `heading-sm`, then one `kr-tab` per artifact, then `kr-clipboard-sheet`, a sheet of `card` laid over the tabs. A web page is `kr-print`, a white printout taped to the sheet: it has its own design, so it stays flat and keeps its own colours in both themes.

Older versions use a stepper of bare marks with `Courier` 11 between them. Beside it is the pin, `kr-pinmark`: a pushpin drawn in pen with Pin by hand. Pressed, it turns `red` and reads Pinned, the kind line adds “pinned to Artifacts”, the artifact's `kr-tab` carries a small `kr-tack`, and the artifact shows up on the Artifacts page; see `Artifacts`. Actions under the sheet are 26px `kr-btn`.
