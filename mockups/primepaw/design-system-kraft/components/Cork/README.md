# Cork

`kr-cork` is the only surface that isn't paper or cardboard: a framed cork face for pinned paper. `kr-tack` is the red pin. Use it for pinned cards and nothing else: sticky notes on a Kanban board, the idea board, and the knowledge base’s graph, which react-force-graph lays out on a canvas over the cork, where each note is a `kr-gcard` index card with its folder colour along the top, strung to the notes it touches with `kr-thread` in `kr-threads` (red for links, pale string for related, dashed ink for the parent). The note you are on takes the highlighter.

On the idea board the cork has no columns. Each sticky note is pinned where you left it with a `kr-tack` and its own slight tilt, and the one you touch comes to the top. A note is written in markdown in Caveat at 20px: a click turns it into the text you wrote, in the same hand, with five note colours as dots and a bin under a dashed rule; Escape or a click away pins it back rendered (headings at 28, 24 and 20px, bullets, numbers, tick boxes you press on the note, a quote with a pencil rule, a dashed rule, bold, italic, strike, Courier code and links in `blue-2`). At night the notes keep their colour, dimmed, and their dark ink.
