Rich text editor
Contenteditable. Bold, italic, inline code and links; ⌘B / ⌘I work natively. Selecting text pops a floating format bar above the selection. ↵ sends, ⇧↵ breaks a line.

Model + effort pill
Model dot colour, name, and a 5-bar effort meter. Click opens a dialog: model rows, then a 5-detent slider. The pill is the only place effort is shown while typing.

Context gauge
A fuel gauge: F is a fresh conversation, E is a full context. The arc is what's left. A ghost segment shows what the draft will burn. Click opens the breakdown by kind.
4
YOLO switch
Real role="switch". On: amber, lightning in the knob, composer border and send button follow. Placeholder changes to say prompts are skipped.
5
Key hint
Mono, dim, hidden under 640px. Gains “select to format” once there is text so the format bar is discoverable.
6
Send
Dormant grey until there is text, then pops to the accent with a soft shadow. On send the arrow flies out the top and returns from below. While the agent works it becomes a still red stop square, no pulse, since it is an exit not an invitation; click or ⎋ halts the turn and the capsule settles to “Stopped”.

The Max-effort moment

Sliding to the fifth detent is the one place the composer is allowed to show off. Everything below runs at once and stays on while effort is Max, so the state is unmistakable even with the dialog closed.

Two shock rings burst from the pill the instant the thumb lands, 120ms apart.
Slider ignites. Fill becomes a flame gradient scrolling at 1.4s; thumb goes cream with a hot halo and pulses 12%.
Seven sparks rise 36px from the thumb on a 1.1s loop, staggered 150ms, each on its own drift.
Conic ring orbits the composer border every 4s with a blurred twin behind it at 28%.
Pill gains a flame + MAX tag in flickering gradient text; the effort bars pulse in sequence.
Copy changes. Placeholder reads “Max effort engaged. Give it the hard one…” in the same gradient; the send button goes molten.

Rules

One popover at a time. A transparent scrim button closes it; Escape closes from the editor.
Colours come from the thread. Model dots, breakdown rows and the gauge reuse the capsule's kind palette; amber is reserved for YOLO and warnings.
Nothing animates without a cause. Only Max effort and the live capsule loop; every other motion answers an input.
Reduced motion collapses every animation and transition to 10ms; states stay readable by colour and copy alone.
Keyboard. Pills, switch, slider, send and the scrim are real buttons and inputs; the format bar is a toolbar; the gauge carries its value in aria-label.
